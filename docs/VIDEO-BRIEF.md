# Future Tabosmart video brief

Storyboard only, updated 8 September 2026 for Tabosmart 1.3.0 source. No video has been produced, recorded, purchased or published by this brief. The 1.3.0 source QA and seven synthetic capture surfaces are verified; the ten-state toolbar image is a labeled static mock. These results do not establish real model inference or authorize future production. [Verification methods](QA.md)

**Purpose:** Show the move from keeping many open tasks in mind to making one understandable decision. The intended feeling is relief with control. Lead with the person's problem, show genuine completed work, and end with visible proof of their choice.

**Format:** About 45–50 seconds, landscape product walkthrough. Warm paper and deep green; clear system typography; calm pacing. Use the actual final interface and readable pointer movement. Avoid an exaggerated alarmed “before” and a magically empty “after.”

History progress may be shown only from a real processing pass or a plainly labeled simulation. Do not imply lifetime coverage, attention measurement or that hashed records are anonymous. A toolbar signal needs actual core/history/AI work; a still image cannot establish continued background inference.

Keep explanation wording concise enough to read at normal speed. Use simple cuts and gentle focus changes, with no looping sparkle, fake scan stages, artificial typing, time-saving claim or urgency. The product should make sense with narration muted. Plan captions and a static-poster alternative; do not make motion carry essential meaning.

If genuine eligible-device AI inference is still unverified when production begins, the main story can demonstrate deterministic names and the complete core flow. A separately labeled source simulation is development evidence, not footage of a real model doing the work. Model-download time should never be concealed behind a fabricated instant-success sequence.

The source ideas and their limits are in [Design principles](DESIGN-PRINCIPLES.md). These are our product hypotheses, not an author endorsement. Before any future capture, use the final [QA record](QA.md), [real first-run boundary](REAL-FIRST-RUN-QA.md) and asset provenance. This brief authorizes no browser-policy workaround, model reset or publication.
