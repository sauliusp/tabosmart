# Design principles for Tabosmart

Updated 8 September 2026 for Tabosmart 1.3.0 source.

The intended feeling is: “I do not have to hold all of these tabs in my head. I can see what was checked, choose one small action, and keep the rest.” Relief, care and control are design goals, not measured outcomes or promises that every user will feel them.

## Source scope

This note draws on public primary sources: Penguin's description and authorized sample of Rory Sutherland's _Alchemy_, plus published interviews with Sutherland. It is not a claim to have read the full book. Product applications below are our design inferences, not Sutherland's recommendations for this extension or an endorsement.

## Product behavior

**Arrival:** A crowded workspace receives a plain acknowledgement of the real tab count and a clear starting point. Fresh installations begin after Chrome grants the required permissions, with a nonblocking disclosure. Stored pauses stay paused. History insights and optional AI have visible controls. Model setup remains a separate gesture; default-on never means already ready.

**While checking:** Display the actual evaluation state. Keep cached results distinguishable from a new completed check. A quick check should finish quickly. Do not add a minimum wait, step through fictitious analysis phases, fabricate a percentage, or claim to understand page contents. Model preparation is a separate state and cannot obscure working tab suggestions.

**After checking:** Show a concise completed-work summary grounded in the snapshot. Counts must name their scope, avoid double-counting overlapping exclusions and describe suggestions as proposals. The [engine contract](ARCHITECTURE.md) distinguishes raw duplicate sets, eligible review copies, candidate proposals and visible cards. Guarded tabs are counted as a unique union; individual exclusion reasons overlap. Insufficient-history counts can include recent use, not only missing history. Show failures as failures, not empty success. If nothing meets the rules, explain that outcome and offer a useful next step, such as reviewing open tabs or returning after more observed use. Never imply that an absence of suggestions means all work is unimportant or already tidy.

**While deciding:** Use a recognizable, metadata-grounded name rather than an invented theme. The deterministic fallback appears immediately. Available local AI may improve the label before review, but open review fields and user edits stay fixed. Put useful choices first without hiding valid proposals. Give the person a clear way to leave things alone or close the workspace and return to browsing.

**After acting:** Confirm only completed actions, with actual counts and partial failures. The dismissible completed-action confirmation should visibly match the native group, saved shelf entry or closed-URL recovery result. Partial failures must stay clear beside the completed count. The emotional payoff comes from seeing that a chosen action worked, not a confetti celebration or a claim that all clutter is solved.

**History and toolbar:** Comprehensive available-history analysis uses real processed counts and partial/completed state. Visit recency/frequency can help ordering, not determine importance. Static toolbar work signals reflect actual checking, history processing or reported AI work and stop when that report ends. Hashing must never be described as anonymity or encryption.

**Motion:** Gentle state changes can direct attention to real work and its result. They must not prolong work, imply unmeasured progress or run indefinitely after completion. Honor reduced-motion preferences; static text and status remain sufficient. Avoid decorative movement competing with names, reasons or decisions.

## Review questions

- Can a person with roughly 100 tabs tell whether Tabosmart has checked them and what the result means?
- Can they distinguish tab checking, Chrome model availability and a single AI request outcome?
- Can they predict an action's scope, change their mind and verify the actual result?
- Does no-results copy explain why there is no proposal and provide a useful next step?
- Does the experience remain understandable without motion, available AI or an urge to close everything?
