LOCAL BY DESIGN

Your browsing stays here.

Local observation starts after installation. Tabosmart uses open-tab titles, URLs and activity, plus available Chrome history, to find and order suggestions. It never reads page contents.

Tabosmart keeps its records in this Chrome profile and sends no browsing data to a server. There are no extension accounts or analytics. Pause observation, turn off history insights or erase Tabosmart’s records in Settings & privacy.

What is stored locally?

Open-tab metadata, recorded URL use, opening relationships, grouping choices, protected URLs, dismissals and saved or recoverable URLs. The history index keeps hashed URL identifiers and aggregate visit evidence, not history titles or full visit lists. Hashes are not anonymous or encrypted. Local records are not a backup; uninstalling removes them.

A COMPLEMENT, NOT A REQUIREMENT

A little local AI. Entirely optional.

Core suggestions work without AI. On supported Chrome devices, local AI can suggest more connections between tabs, group names and clearer wording. You still review every action.

Local assistance is enabled by default for new workspaces. Chrome may need an initial model download. Model input is processed on your device, with no cloud fallback. Availability depends on Chrome, your device and language.
