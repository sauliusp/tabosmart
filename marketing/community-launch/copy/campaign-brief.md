# A place for your open possibilities

The behavioral idea, inspired by themes in Rory Sutherland’s Alchemy, is to reduce the perceived cost of closing a tab. People can be reluctant to let go of an intention even when keeping the page open no longer helps. Lead with permission to keep the possibility for later, backed by actual saved URLs and reviewed actions. This is an original campaign application, not a quotation or endorsement.

The proof follows the promise: actual suggestions, reasons, selected actions and a local shelf. No invented hours saved, performance uplift, user numbers or testimonials. Recovery restores URLs, not unsaved work. Privacy reduces a real hesitation; optional AI stays in a supporting role.

The five-image sequence is: relief, a place for later, control, local AI, community participation. The video follows the same order with the maker’s own authorized voice reference, exact interface captures, natural speech and a readable ending.

Feedback starts with a concrete situation: what were you doing, what happened, what did you expect? Invite ideas and problems in one visible public board. Do not gate feedback by satisfaction, discourage honest reviews, offer rewards, or manufacture votes. Close the loop by replying to real reports and linking fixes in release notes; make no unattended response-time promises.

Website discovery: four distinct, useful guides linked from the homepage; rendered text, clear concise answers, individual titles and canonical URLs, honest application/article structured data, captions, video transcript, sitemap and robots. No doorway-page expansion or speculative AI-only markup. After the final domain is chosen, update all canonicals and sitemap entries together, redirect the old hostname if hosting supports it, and verify Search Console. Public indexing and traffic are not guaranteed.
