Nomination rationale:

Tabosmart helps people make a few useful decisions about an overloaded tab bar. It brings related work together, makes repeated URLs easier to review, and saves links for later. Every grouping or closing action is reviewed by the user. Suggestions explain the evidence behind them, and users can change a proposed name or selection before acting.

A short installation guide answers why the extension is useful and shows where to start. The workspace includes clear privacy controls, website favicons, keyboard-accessible controls, and an optional community feedback link. Core features work without an account or payment. Browsing records stay in the Chrome profile. Optional local AI offers additional help on supported devices, while the standard suggestions remain useful when AI is unavailable.

The listing uses actual interface screenshots, a concise narrated demonstration, and specific explanations of privacy and recovery limits. Tabosmart 0.3.0 is the first feedback release, with user experiences guiding the path to 1.0.0. It is being developed with community feedback, with a public place for people to describe the task they were trying to complete and suggest improvements.

First maker comment:

Hi, I'm Saulius, the maker of Tabosmart.

A tab often stays open because closing it feels like losing a possibility. I wanted a calmer way to decide what deserves space now and what can wait.

Tabosmart brings related work together, helps you review repeated pages, and saves links for later. You review each action. Nothing groups or closes without your approval. Recovery reopens URLs, so it cannot restore an unsaved form or edit.

Core suggestions work without AI. On supported Chrome devices, optional local AI can help find connections and suggest names. Its model inputs stay on your device.

This is an early release, and I'd like to build it around real browsing habits. What were you trying to do when your tabs became difficult to manage? Which suggestion helped, and which one missed the point?
